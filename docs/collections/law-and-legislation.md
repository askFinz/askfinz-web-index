# Case law and legislation search

Judgments from national and supranational courts, and the acts, regulations and statutory instruments beside them — searchable by what a case decided or what

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/collections/law-and-legislation](https://askfinz.com/collections/law-and-legislation)

## Key points

### What you can do with it

Search by the point of law — Describe the issue and find the judgments that turned on it, without knowing the case name.

### Filed to answer

Which judgments have turned on this issue, when I do not know a single case name?

### Written to

A new record is a newly published document. A revision replaces the text on the record that already exists rather than adding a second one, so the count does not double.

### Where it stops

It is not legal advice, and a judgment found is not a judgment that binds you.
