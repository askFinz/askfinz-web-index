# News archive search across thousands of publishers

The day's coverage read as it is published, with outlets running the same wire copy recognised as one story.

**Canonical page:** [https://askfinz.com/collections/news-and-briefings](https://askfinz.com/collections/news-and-briefings)

## Key points

### What you can do with it

Follow a story across outlets — See who covered what, side by side, instead of one account at a time.

### Filed to answer

Who else reported this, and did any of them add reporting of their own?

### Written to

A new record is a newly published article. A revision replaces the text on the record that already exists rather than adding a second one, so the count does not double.

### Where it stops

It does not adjudicate. Where outlets disagree about what happened, both accounts are kept and nothing here marks one of them true.
