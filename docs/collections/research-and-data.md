## Key points

### What you can do with it

Trace a claim to its study — Find the work a figure came from, not an article summarising it.

### Filed to answer

Which studies tested this in adults over 65, and how many people did they enrol?

### Written to

A new record is a newly published work. A revision replaces the text on the record that already exists rather than adding a second one, so the count does not double.

### Where it stops

Subscription journals. What is held is open-access work and the metadata publishers release — a paywalled paper is not here in full text.
